# ATLAS | YC-style go-to-market playbook

## Positioning

Don't sell “SEO for ChatGPT” or “we will make your salon #1 in AI”. Sell **proof of visibility and a controlled improvement process**.

> “When prospective customers ask AI for beauty services in Prague, do you know which businesses get mentioned? We show the answers, competitors and cited sources, fix verified gaps in your online presence, and measure what changes.”

## Wedge

First 30 days: one niche, one city, one person making decisions. We suggest owner-managed beauty/wellness salons in Prague. They have recognizable services and conversion events (appointments), though pricing must be proven in interviews. Other verticals can wait.

### Why a small wedge

- Easy to generate 10-20 *genuine* purchase/discovery questions per category.
- Service catalog and neighborhood context are relatively concrete.
- Owner can approve factual listing and content changes fast.
- Referral enquiries can sometimes be tracked, which matters more than mentions alone.

## Sell before building further

Day 1: manually audit 10 businesses using 8 consistent unbranded questions, comparable locale and methods. Keep full evidence, limitations and date. Do not make claims from a single stochastic sample. Identify 2-3 genuine data/website gaps per business.

Days 2-3: speak to 10 owners and show their real evidence records, not demo results. Ask how many new customers AI referrals would need to deliver to be worthwhile, and how they measure new appointments.

Days 4-7: offer 3 paid **baseline + remediation** pilots. Maintain human approval. Use the local product for research, but never hand out localhost access or expose client data as a public SaaS.

Weeks 2-4: implement fixes, add recurring observations, record actual enquiries, ask for willingness to renew. Stop if owners won't pay for evidence and corrective work.

## Pricing hypotheses to test, in CZK

| Service | Target experimental price | Deliverable |
|---|---:|---|
| Baseline AI visibility audit | 3,990 one-off | Prompt set, raw evidence, citations, gaps, actions |
| Evidence-backed setup sprint | 14,900 one-off | Owner-approved improvements to accessible listings/site |
| Continued monitoring | 4,990 per month | Consistent observations, review, report and action updates |

These are **offers to test**, not proven Czech market rates. External software/API cost and work hours must be tracked by client. Avoid fixed price promises if remediation depends on a developer or platform the client doesn't control.

## Pilot definition

- Measurement cohort: 12-20 unbranded intent prompts, separately labelled by language, service and approximate location.
- Evidence: at least 3 comparable observations per prompt where budget permits, on both sides of a change; do not state statistical significance without proper design.
- Repairs: only verifiable name/address/hours/service details, technical crawlability and authorized site updates.
- Outcome: mention/citation coverage plus client-provided leads/bookings with attribution limitations.
- Commercial checkpoint: 3 paid pilots from first 20-30 qualified conversations, then at least 2 renewal agreements. These are proposed founder targets, not predicted results.

## What creates defensibility

Not dashboards or tokenized “visibility scores”. Defensibility may develop from an unusual dataset of location and language-specific observations, signed remediation/measurement history, productized implementation workflows, agency distribution, and client-authorized booking attribution. None is a defensible moat on day one.

## Acquisition channels to test

Founder-led walk-ins to targeted independent businesses, owner introductions, referrals from web developers and small digital agencies, and tightly scoped audit-led outreach. Respect Czech/EU marketing rules and privacy, particularly for contact information.

## Founder metrics

Paid pilots, owner meetings to sale conversion, time to verified first improvement, monthly gross margin per account, evidence run cost, response validity rate, customer retention, and incremental enquiry evidence. Do not celebrate high AI mention rates if no customer uses or pays for the insights.

## Investor story when earned

“Businesses are becoming discoverable through agents and AI answers rather than only ranked blue links. ATLAS is the system that measures that discovery, turns it into approved changes, and ties it to commercial outcomes.”

That story is a hypothesis until paying customers, stable instrumentation and measurable retention exist.
